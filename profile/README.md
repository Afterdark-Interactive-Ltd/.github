## About us

Afterdark Interactive is an independent UK game studio and publisher creating original experiences, supporting developers, and building long-term gaming communities.

We care about distinctive worlds, systems that create memorable stories, and the technology that helps communities thrive around them. Our work spans original game development, publishing support, roleplay experiences, and community-management infrastructure.

## What we're building

|Project|What it is|Status|
|-|-|-|
|**Depth Runners**|A cooperative PC adventure through vast, procedurally generated cave systems, built around exploration, teamwork, and the tension of pushing deeper.|In development|
|[**Afterdark Sentinel**](https://sentinel.afterdarkinteractive.co.uk)|Shared moderation infrastructure for independent Discord communities, combining local control with network intelligence, cases, appeals, tickets, automod, and member tools.|In development|
|[**Underground Roleplay**](https://undergroundrp.net)|A serious, economy-based FiveM roleplay community focused on character-first progression, original systems, and stories shaped by its players.|Live community|

## Our focus

* **Original games** — distinctive, systems-led experiences designed to be played and remembered together.
* **Publishing** — practical support for developers with strong ideas and a clear creative identity.
* **Communities** — player-first spaces built for meaningful stories and long-term growth.
* **Community technology** — secure tools that help independent teams operate, moderate, and collaborate.
